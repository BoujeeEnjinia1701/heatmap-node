# BOM notes

Costs are indicative USD prices at quantity 1 for a TRL 2 concept, to be replaced with quoted prices at TRL 3.

- Line numbers 1 to 9 match the callouts in `media/exploded.png`. Line 10 has no callout.
- Sensor head (lines 2 to 10): about $120, equal to the `budget_usd` of $120 with no margin.
- Full node including the FieldNode core (line 1, about $126 from the FieldNode BOM): about $246, about twice the $120 budget. How to count the shared core against this budget is proposed, awaiting Amish (see `docs/REVIEW.md`).
- The street pole, gateway (TwinKit or a public LoRaWAN network), installation labor and traffic management are not included.
